# Bug Report

## Summary
**Title**: A spectating client's 3D view is black because its own body falls below the map
**ID**: BUG-0005
**Severity**: S2-High
**Priority**: P2-Fix next sprint
**Status**: Open
**Reported**: 2026-10-15
**Reporter**: HUD-4 agent, while capturing the spectating screenshot (hud-results story 004)

## Classification
- **Category**: Gameplay / Rendering
- **System**: match-flow (spectators, story 007) and the client round scene (story 011)
- **Frequency**: Always, for any Eliminated or Escaped survivor's client
- **Regression**: No. No spectator camera has existed since spectators were added.

## Environment
- **Build**: `e3d8a9d` (HUD-4 merge)
- **Platform**: Windows 11, Godot 4.7.2, windowed client from `tests/manual/round_view.gd --bot=ownstate`
- **Scene/Level**: Research Facility greybox

## Reproduction Steps
**Preconditions**: a round with at least one Eliminated survivor.

1. Start `tests/manual/round_host.gd` and `tests/manual/round_view.gd --bot=ownstate`, ports 30740 and up.
2. Let the shown survivor become Eliminated, so its client starts spectating.

**Expected Result**: the client shows the watched survivor's view, with the spectating HUD over it.
**Actual Result**: the HUD is correct, but the 3D view behind it is black.

## Cause (from the HUD-4 report; not re-verified by the orchestrator)
- A spectator's own snapshot entry carries the watched survivor's state.
- `OwnerPredictor` keeps replaying the spectator's unacknowledged inputs on top of that position.
- The client body falls below the map, to about y -18000 after 10 s.
- The client has no spectator camera that follows the watched body.

## Evidence
- `production/qa/evidence/own-state-and-hud-states/spectating.png`

## Fix direction
- Stop owner prediction while spectating.
- Put the camera on the watched survivor's interpolated body.
- Hide the spectator's own body.
